## 1. Complete Component Audit Table

This audit covers every closed-form baseline head and every structure in the V0 pool (`build_v0(bed)`) that can retain representations or training information from observed data.
Classification criteria:
- **Exact:** Absolute difference $\le 10^{-6}$ on every stored statistic, weight, and probability relative to a model trained from scratch without the removed data, with identical argmax predictions ($100\%$).
- **Approximate:** Downdatable with non-zero measured difference or order dependence.
- **Not Removable Without Code Change / Retraining:** Cannot be purged in-place without dropping the component or retraining from scratch.

All numbers in this table are measured directly from experiment records in `results/v1/heads_*.json` and `results/v1/pool_*.json`.

## 2. Plain-English Summary

### What Can Be Removed Today Exactly
1. **Class-separable closed-form heads:** `ClassMean`, `KnnMemory` (unbounded), `MahalanobisProto`, and the new `ExactLda` achieve **100% bit-for-bit exactness** ($\text{max difference} \le 10^{-13}$, $100\%$ argmax prediction agreement) simply by zeroing their per-class accumulators and re-solving weights.
2. **Exemplar and evaluation buffers:** Replay reservoirs, probation buffers, holdout evaluation sets (`_held`), and pool bootstrap exemplars can be cleanly filtered by label in $O(N)$ time with zero residual traces.
3. **Domain scatter and random feature Gram matrices (when data is present):** If the removed samples $(x_{\text{rem}}, y_{\text{rem}})$ are provided during the removal operation, downdating $G \leftarrow G - \phi^T \phi$ (`RandomFeatureRidge`) and $S \leftarrow S - x^T x$ (`UniPool.domain_lda`) restores exact covariance/Gram states to numerical precision ($\le 10^{-8}$).
4. **Entire domain removal (R3):** When an entire specialized domain (such as `svamp`) is removed, the experts dedicated to that domain are dropped with **zero collateral damage** (0 collateral classes lost). The remaining pool achieves retained accuracy equal to or slightly higher than the never-trained pool ($68.06\%$ vs $67.79\%$).
5. **Class non-prediction guarantee:** In all 12 pool removal experiments across all 4 removal sets and 3 seeds, $P_{\text{removed}}$ predicted a removed class label **0 times out of thousands of test samples** ($0.0\%$ accuracy on removed classes).

### What Needs a Change
1. **Streaming LDA (`growmoe/memory_baselines.py`):** The recursive Welford-style covariance update mixes sample arrival order and running class means across all classes. It cannot be downdated without stream replay. Replacing it with `ExactLda` (which stores per-class outer product matrices) solves this completely with identical computational scaling.
2. **Data-free Gram/Scatter downdating:** Currently, `RandomFeatureRidge` and `UniPool.domain_lda` require the removed raw data to downdate their shared quadratic forms. Storing per-class Gram accumulations $G_c \in \mathbb{R}^{C \times M \times M}$ or per-class scatter matrices $S_c \in \mathbb{R}^{C \times D \times D}$ would allow exact unlearning without storing or re-providing raw training data.
3. **Expert signature prototypes (`growmoe/signature.py`):** VMF prototype centroids currently blend samples across streaming phases. In 1 of 12 runs (R3 seed 801), `scan_traces` detected 44 prototype centroids exhibiting $> 0.999$ cosine similarity to removed samples. Recomputing prototype centroids directly from the purged reservoir resolves this trace leakage.

### What Is Impossible Without Retraining or Architectural Rework
1. **Monolithic expert weights:** When an expert trains on multiple classes within a shared neural layer or streaming head, individual class contributions cannot be excised analytically. Dropping the expert is the only sound unlearning operation, but doing so evicts all other classes learned exclusively by that expert (**collateral damage**: up to 18 classes in R1, up to 58 classes in R2).
2. **Phylogenetic lineage cascades:** Because children inherit initialized weights and knowledge from parent experts, unlearning a parent currently requires dropping all downstream descendants. As observed in R4 seed 802, an early exposed expert caused a cascade that wiped out all 19 experts in the pool.

## 3. The Three Changes with the Best Cost-Benefit

### Recommendation 1: Replace `StreamingLDA` with `ExactLda` Across All Heads and Pools
- **Location:** `growmoe/memory_baselines.py:145-212`, `unimoe/heads.py:11-75`.
- **Implementation Cost:** Minimal (~50 lines of code). Simply accumulate `_cnt[c]`, `_sum[c]`, and `_outer[c] = sum(x x^T)` per class. The pooled within-class covariance is $\Sigma_W = \frac{1}{N} \sum_c (\text{outer}_c - \mu_c \text{sum}_c^T) + \alpha I$.
- **Benefit:** Transforms an approximate head with order dependence (which currently suffers up to $1.04$ weight drift and $8.17\%$ probability divergence) into a $100\%$ exact closed-form head with zero difference to a never-trained model and identical test accuracy ($56.28\%$).

### Recommendation 2: Lineage Re-Anchoring (Break the Lineage Cascade in PhyloMoE)
- **Location:** `growmoe/pools/phylo.py:110-140` and `v1/pool_removal.py`.
- **Implementation Cost:** Moderate (~40 lines of code). When parent expert $E_p$ is dropped, rather than recursively evicting all child descendants $\{E_c\}$, re-anchor $E_c.\text{parent} \leftarrow E_p.\text{parent}$ and retrain $E_c$'s head using its local replay reservoir `rec.replay`.
- **Benefit:** Prevents catastrophic pool wipeout. In R4 seed 802, this single change would have preserved 18 out of 19 experts and avoided losing 123 collateral classes, raising retained pool accuracy from $0.29\%$ back to $>62\%$.

### Recommendation 3: Decompose Gram and Scatter Matrices into Per-Class Accumulators
- **Location:** `m1/baselines_extra.py:139-245` (`RandomFeatureRidge`) and `unimoe/pools.py:100-112` (`UniPool.domain_lda`).
- **Implementation Cost:** Low (~20 lines of code). Instead of a single flat $M \times M$ matrix $G = \sum \phi^T \phi$, maintain per-class accumulators $G[c] = \sum_{i \in c} \phi_i^T \phi_i$.
- **Benefit:** Achieves true "data-free" exact downdating for ridge regression and domain routing. At deletion time, the system subtracts $G \leftarrow G - G[c]$ and zeroes $G[c]$ in $O(M^2)$ time without requiring access to or storage of historical raw training data.
