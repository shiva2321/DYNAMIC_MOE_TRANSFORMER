## A4-7 in full: the founding cluster counted twice (E-037)

While looking for a fix of the infant window (the user's decision of 2026-10-16) I found that the founding cluster of every new expert enters its signature twice, which wrecks the new signature's calibration for about seven batches; the x4 infant relaxation hides that for one batch and blinds the pool to a new regime for four. The fix is built behind a flag that is OFF (stage 4.1). It changes every arm of the experiment in the same way, but the stage-4 constants (`grow_gap`, the router setting, the transfer margin) were chosen on cells run with the doubled signature and the pre-registration was written against them.

The dev check is done (E-037, `RESULTS_S3_DEDUPE.md`): over 41 cell pairs (function-regime guard streams, arithmetic operator-by-operator, banking77, one-function arithmetic) the fix with `infant_relax` 1 changes accuracy by +0.36 [-0.06, +0.79] points, worst pair -4.4, best +4.0, with the same expert counts; the gain of the full stack (S3+T+G) over S3 on arithmetic is +8.7 points with the fix against +8.3 without it, so the stage-4 gains do not depend on the defect. It is safe on the dev streams; it is not shown to help there (their regimes are long, which is where the infant window cannot matter; in the harness it finds more regimes at 2 batches per regime).

Recommendation (my call, reversible): keep the confirmatory run on the current defaults, and confirm the twin fix in its own small pre-registered step on fresh seeds right after (bars to be written first: as many regimes found and mean accuracy not lower at regimes of 10 and 5 batches, no cascade with `infant_relax` 1, a gain or a tie at 3 and 2 batches), then flip the default and pin the old entry points. Reasons: (a) folding it in now would change the base of all four arms after the constants were chosen and the bars were written against the doubled signature, although the dev check says the effect is small; (b) a separate step keeps a failure of one from hiding a result of the other; (c) nothing is lost by waiting, since the fix is neutral where the stage-4 claims are made.
