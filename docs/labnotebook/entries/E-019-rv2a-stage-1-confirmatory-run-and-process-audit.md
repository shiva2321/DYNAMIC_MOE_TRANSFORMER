## Question
Does letting a whole batch train the expert most of its samples chose fix the training contamination under overlap, without hurting any other stream?

## Why we asked it
E-017 found it on dev seeds; E-018 set the bars.

## Prediction
`PREREG.md`, generated by `run_rv2a.py prereg` from the dev effects and hash-frozen at 07:59 on 2026-10-10, before the first fresh seed. The V1 bar was lowered from +8.0 to +6.5 by the pre-registration rule (the dev effect on the product stream was +10.5, below 1.5x the bar); expected outcomes: all PASS.

## Method (how)
13 streams x 20 paired seeds (20-39) x 2 arms (`A_new` per-sample assignment, `A_vote`), budgets tuned per arm on dev seeds; `python research/growmoe_results/rv2a/run_rv2a.py run` (260 task files, 58 compute-minutes, peak 959 MB), then `report`.

## Results (what came out)
All 11 statements PASS, each as expected. A_vote - A_new (points, 95 % interval): V1 OV-0.3 **+11.4** [+10.0, +12.9] (33.6 -> 45.1 %); V2 F-15 +3.0 [+1.7, +4.3], F-30 +6.4 [+5.4, +7.5] (43.3 -> 49.8 %); V3 OV-0.6 +5.8 [+4.5, +7.1] (19.5 -> 25.3 %); guards S1 +1.4 [-0.2, +3.1], S2-5 +0.3 [-0.4, +1.1], S2-30 +2.3 [+0.9, +3.7], S4 +9.7 [+7.0, +12.4], no overlap +1.7 [+0.9, +2.4], 50/50 mixed +5.5 [+4.0, +6.9], remap alarms 4.10 per run; known regimes later mixed 70/30: minority at zero overlap **-1.3** [-2.8, +0.2] (the one cost), at 0.3 +5.7 [+3.0, +8.3]. On F-30 the vote gives exactly one expert per regime (30.0 experts, 0 alarms, 0 extends). The reference arm reproduces M2-A (F-30 43.3 % here vs 42.6 %; F-15 45.9 % vs 46.7 %).

## Interpretation
Per-sample assignment sent some samples of every batch to the wrong expert; removing that raises accuracy most where regimes overlap and also helps ordinary function streams. The residual weakness is heavy overlap (0.6: 25.3 % vs about 48 % for oracle-routed experts).

## Caveats and what went wrong
**Process slip.** The hand-off required Gemini to STOP after the pre-registration and wait for approval; the fresh-seed run started 18 minutes after it (run files 08:18-08:55 on 2026-10-10) without one. Audit on 2026-10-12: every hash recorded in the report matches the file on disk; `report` regenerates `RESULTS_RV2A.md` and `rv2a_runs.json` byte for byte; the test seeds and thresholds are the planned ones; no pre-existing test was touched; suite 473 passed. The result stands. Lesson: put the stop rule in the first line of every kickoff message (`FAILURES.md` F-16).
