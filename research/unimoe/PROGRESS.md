## Protocol (fixed now)
- **Seeds.** 499 = tuning only (per-arm recipe grids, sequential schedule, metric = mean final regime accuracy). 500-502 = the first evaluation (all schedules, all systems); 503-504 are added for the robustness block; 600-604 are NOT used.
- **Arms and recipes.** Every trainable arm is tuned on seed 499: UniMoE variants over inner_steps in {1, 5, 20}; `U-recipe` over inner_steps in {5, 20, 50} x replay_size in {256, 1024}; `replay_eqmem` over width in {256, 1024, 4096} x steps in {1, 5, 20} at the memory of the tuned `U-real` pool; `finetune` and `joint` over steps in {5, 20}. The oracle experts use the tuned `finetune` steps. The tuned recipe is then fixed for all evaluation seeds and printed with every table.
- **Reading.** Final mean accuracy = mean over the 22 regimes of the accuracy after the last phase (arithmetic regimes scored per digit slot; exact match per problem is reported separately). Forgetting, transfer, structure, routing, novelty, cost, failure analysis as in the design, section 6. Intervals: 95 % over seeds; with three seeds they are wide and are read as such.

### E5 result and what it found (2026-10-30)
E5 as declared: thresholds 0.25 / 0.35 / 0.50 in the mixed schedule gave results IDENTICAL to the default (0.502 / 0.509 / 0.519, one expert, zero growth events): both declared predictions MISSED. The cause is not the threshold. `GapMonitor.mature(grow_min_age = 300, ...)` requires an expert to have trained on 300 batches of its own, and the whole mixed stream is 162 batches of 64 (the sequential stream 221, at most 23 per regime), so **the growth rule cannot fire on streams this short, by construction**. I had not considered the maturity condition when I wrote the E5 and E4 predictions. Consequences: (1) "growth never fired" in this evaluation is a property of my stream length against the policy's defaults, NOT evidence that the mechanism fails; (2) the evaluation so far says nothing about automatic width growth; (3) the shadow-trial policy of `U-deep` (growth_rule probe) is bound by a similar age and was identical to U-real for the same reason.

### E5b result and the diagnosis of why growth is dead (2026-10-30)
E5b (fast maturity, thresholds 0.17 / 0.35 / 0.50, mixed schedule, 20 steps): identical to the default again, zero growth events, all three declared predictions MISSED. Reading the gating code (`phylo._gap_growth_step`, `gapgrow.GapMonitor`) and measuring the expert's monitor at the end of a mixed run (seed 500; `/tmp` diagnostic, numbers copied here): the fit gap is 0.093 / 0.591 / 0.981 at 1 / 5 / 20 inner steps for a 128-unit expert and 0.045 / 0.388 / 0.821 for a 16-unit one; growth (with the fast settings and threshold 0.5) fired once at 1 and 5 steps and never at 20. **The gap is the share of a batch's loss that the expert's own steps remove, so it measures how many steps the expert gets, not how much capacity it has; the declared threshold 0.17 means "starved" only at about one step per batch. The tuned recipe (20 steps) therefore disables width growth for every plausible threshold, and the default maturity age (300 batches) disables it again on streams shorter than 300 batches.** Two couplings that the earlier studies could not see because their recipes used few steps. This is a finding about the system (a growth rule whose statistic depends on the training recipe), not about my streams.

## P4: the four improvements (2026-10-30; the user said "test and run and analyze and improve all 4 and report once done")
Built and tested first (`unimoe/pools.py`, `heads.py`, `systems2.py`, `capacity.py`, `refusal.py`; 17 new tests, 7 of 7 pool mutants caught; one design flaw found by an integration test: a domain head as an LDA over domain ids is a poor model of a domain that is a union of clusters, so it is an LDA over the class labels summed within each domain's block):
 1. **Growth read from a one-step probe** (`UG1`): the share of a fresh batch's loss that ONE step on a copy of the expert removes; independent of the inner steps; maturity scaled to the stream (30 / 20 / 20); tested on a stream that needs capacity (six arithmetic operators, 10,000 examples each: `capacity.py`) and as a no-harm check on the main suite.
 2. **Domain head** (`UDOM` hard, `UDOMS` soft): a running LDA over the class labels, summed within each domain's label block, trained for free from the labels and fused into the answer.
 3. **Refusal layer** (`refusal.py`, experiment `p4_refusal.py`): the pool's novelty score and the asking reader's, each cut set on a sample of VERIFIED known messages (n = 60, q = 0.05, conformal), fused by "either"; evaluated on held-out known text and the three out-of-distribution sets.
 4. **Expert fit** (`ULDA`, `oracle_lda`): a running LDA head inside every expert, blended into its answer in log-probability; tested first under oracle routing, then in the pool. `UPLUS` = 1 + 2 + 4.
**Protocol.** Seed 499 tunes (per schedule; the growth threshold on the capacity stream), seeds 500-502 evaluate, seeds 503-504 are a confirmation of the final combined system that no choice touched (dev seeds, not the reserved 600-604). Control for every comparison: `UG0` (U-real inside the new class, tested equal to U-real). Recipes of the pool variants = U-real's tuned recipe of the same schedule; the new knobs are tuned on seed 499: growth threshold in {0.02, 0.05, 0.10, 0.20, 0.40}, domain power beta in {0.5, 1, 2}, LDA weight in {0.25, 0.5, 0.75}.
