  4. In-Memory Tree Retrieval: Branch-and-bound tree retrieval in Python is 36x - 60x slower than a
     vectorized dense GPU top-B baseline at N <= 4096.
  5. Kernel Target: Tiled CUDA kernel achieved 2.6x against the engine kernel (unmet against the 4x target).

Final Recommendation (Note N3):
