## 5. Deciding at the level of the batch (patched copy of `PhyloMoE.observe`)

V0 = repaired pool. V1 = every non-novel sample of a batch trains the expert most of them were routed to (if it holds >= 50 % of them). V2 = V1 plus Fisher's combination of the batch's per-sample novelty p-values: if it rejects at 1e-4 the whole batch is novel and goes to probation. Training purity = for each expert, the share of the samples it trained on that came from its dominant regime (summed over experts, divided by all trained samples).
